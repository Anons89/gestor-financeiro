Frequently asked questions

How do I add an expense?

Just type what you spent in the text box — for example, "coffee 3.50" or "uber 12". Algent's AI categorises it automatically.

How does the free trial work?

You get 30 days of full access when you sign up. No credit card required to start. After the trial, it's £2.99/month.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, anytime. On iOS, go to Settings in the app and tap "Manage subscription" to open your Apple subscriptions. On the web, use the "Cancel subscription" option in Settings.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap "Restore purchases". Your subscription will be recognised automatically.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Your data is stored securely with encryption. We never sell your data. Read our privacy policy for full details.

What currencies are supported?

Algent supports GBP (£), EUR (€), USD ($), BRL (R$), JPY (¥), CHF, SEK (kr), NOK (kr), and DKK (kr). You can switch anytime in Settings.
