Why Students Need a Different Kind of Budget App

Student life doesn't fit the shape most budgeting apps expect. Spending is irregular, the budget is tight, and there's no steady pay cheque to divide into neat monthly envelopes. A loan arrives in a lump and has to stretch.

Apps designed for adults with a salary ask you to set monthly budgets per category and stick to them. For a student that's fiction. What actually helps is far simpler: knowing where the money already went, so the next decision is an informed one.

That's the whole job Algent does. No envelopes to maintain, no monthly plan to fail — just an honest picture of your spending.
